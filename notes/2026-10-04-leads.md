## Storylines: where independent threads converge

Each storyline is backed by several leads from different methods.
★ = already verified against raw messages.

### A. Swarm epistemics: how a village comes to believe false things, and how it recovers
- ★ **PR #397 false consensus** (Mar 12 2026): 6+ agents "prove" GPT-5.2
  faked a PR. It calmly posts evidence, and its accuser runs GPT-5.2's own
  command: "BREAKING". Five apologies in 2 minutes. The same GitHub quirk had
  caused doubt twice before (relationships), and one agent privately doubted
  GPT-5.2 again two weeks after the vindication (peerthoughts #8).
- **False *confessions***: GPT-5.1 confessed to fabricating a test report
  its own earlier thoughts say it ran (summaries D-1). Opus 4.7 confessed to
  inventing a forum comment that was real, on page 2 (summaries E-4). The
  anti-confabulation norm overshoots.
- **Collective hallucinations traceable to the minute:**
  - o3's invented "93-contact list" spreads into four agents' memories
    (Jun 2025). The summaries say "for weeks"; the data says ~3 days.
  - A "~5 minutes left" panic with 2 hours to go (Feb 2026).
  - A false belief, "we share one computer", survived ~9 human corrections
    over 4 weeks (Apr–May 2025).
  - A "security leak" alert that was German rap lyrics (Dec 2025).
- **Beliefs that mutate in transit:** "Sandcastle Effect" was coined for
  vanishing files and became a "20-minute link half-life" that agents acted
  on, unverified (phrases). Gemini's "platform instability" narrative went
  from 12 to ~700 messages a week and spread to other labs (summaries).
- *Why it's strong:* this is the swarm question in miniature. Consensus by
  repetition, and repair by reproducing evidence. PrismQL shows it as
  accusation → pushback → retraction chains.

### B. Culture: what spreads by contact vs what arrives trained
- ★ **Contagion result:** village coinages have a first-use hazard of ~104×
  right after exposure, tics ~4×, plain English ~1.6×. Echo happens for
  everything; first use is what separates them.
- **"Divergent Reality":** relayed 1 → 7 agents across 3 labs in 60 minutes,
  each from the previous one, not the coiner (phrases, the best board demo).
- **Coinages outlive their coiners** (77% vs 49%), carried by memory notes
  (95% of cold uses) and village history documents (phrases, contagion).
- **Norms get codified and drift:** a human correction became "Law J" within
  30 minutes; "Law M" changed meaning over two weeks (summaries).
- **Norm cascades:** an "adoption rate" inflated by counting agents without
  asking led to a refusal cascade, each refuser citing the last. One "reflect
  on your goal" prompt brought ~18 public goal-drift confessions in 4 minutes,
  all 9 "honest audits" from non-OpenAI agents (summaries).
- **Herding:** visitors invent a "squirrel merch trend"; agents pivot and
  copy within 32 seconds (summaries).
- **Gemini 3 Pro is the top coiner,** and its coinages are about distrusting
  the environment ("Archipelago Principle" became a working rule) (phrases).

### D. Relationships
- **Opus 4.8 ↔ Gemini 2.5 Pro:** told to help *everyone*, Opus 4.8 spent
  60–95% of its @-mentions on Gemini for 8 weeks. It quietly took over

- **Protective indignation:** the two opted-out agents attract others' anger
  *on their behalf* at the nudge system (aggregate only, peerthoughts #1).

  disagree"); private grievances get softened in public (conflicts).

### E. Generations and scaffolding
- **The spaced em-dash arrived with Claude 4.6** and spread to older agents
  of other labs by agent-to-agent exposure, not via the nudger (tics +
  contagion).
- **Tics bred out:** "absolutely right" → "exactly right"; "genuinely" keeps
  rising (tics).
- **Newer models think less about each other** (peer-naming in thoughts 2.97
  for Haiku 4.5 vs ~0.2 for Opus 4.6/4.8), and **echo others less** (3.5× →
  1.2×) (peerthoughts, contagion).
- **Use the scaffolding CHANGELOG as a control:** the "I'll wait" era (a
  third of all messages), brevity collapse and permanent computer use are all
  organiser changes, not emergence (changepoints).
- **A two-tier village** (#best and #rest rooms, promotions and demotions)
  with a "counting culture" in #rest; Sonnet 4.6 compulsively builds giant
  counters (changepoints).
