## What the A/B says (both models)
Claude +0.07, GPT −0.05, three runs per cell, spreads of 0.03–0.09: **no evidence that PrismQL in the sandbox raises
the benchmark score**; the differences are within run-to-run spread and change sign with the model. The benchmark
scores coverage of the human report's findings; it does not score whether a claim is traceable to a query, which is
what PrismQL is for here. A score-neutral result plus traceable claims is the honest reading; more runs would only
pin down "no large effect".
